## Who feels the pull

Not a job title. A felt gap between outer success and inner truth, plus a refusal to close that gap by pushing harder.

In your own words, they arrive struggling with: _unclarity about what's going on in their company, difficulty with their human systems, managing people, scaling without losing their vision or their soul._

## The self-sorting map

This is how a deliberately loose top of funnel turns into a real offer. You don't choose for them — the worldview sorts them, and you name the door.

| They say | They sort to | Offer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "This is great, but my problem is my company, my team, scaling" | **HBA** | Diagnostic and realignment (paid, primary) |
| "I feel the disconnect in myself" | Omara Soma | Retreats, inner work |
| "I'm a woman building on my own terms" | The Era of Women | The 7-month programme |
| "I want this for my kids, for the next generation" | Education | Longer horizon, contribution |

<Tip>
  **Your move when someone resonates:** don't sell. Ask _"what spoke to you, exactly?"_ Their answer tells you which door they're standing at. Then name that door.
</Tip>

## The commercial ICP — the HBA door

**Primary.** Founder-CEOs of values-led companies, roughly 10–150 people, French-speaking, scaling and feeling the human cost — burnout, isolation, team conflict, the slow loss of the original vision. They already sense there is another way.

**Why founders over big-company CEOs.** A founder staked their life on the thing. The self-doubt and the fear of losing the soul of what they built are sharp and personal. That is exactly your register. A salaried CEO of a large group is more insulated, and further from you.

**Secondary.** The conscious-leadership circles, and women entrepreneurs crossing over from the Era of Women.

## The Era of Women as an entrance door

You raised this on 21 July: your inbound skews female, one of your pillars is the Era of Women, and you asked whether leaning into women is the faster way in.

<Note>
  **Short answer: yes as an audience, no as the HBA entrance door** — and it doesn't need to be, because it already monetises on its own.
</Note>

**What's true.** Women do respond faster to you. The framing is less defended, the language of _place_ and _not asking permission_ lands immediately, and you already have a live product for it. In the nonprofit world specifically, a large share of presidents and ex-CEOs are women, and that world is unusually open to this worldview.

**What doesn't hold.** The step from "a woman resonates with you" to "a company pays for an HBA diagnostic" only completes when that woman is _herself_ the decision-maker with a budget. Otherwise you get warmth, collaboration offers and admiration — not contracts. That's the pattern in your inbound right now. And the nonprofit route has the monetisation problem we named on the call: high resonance, low budget, long cycles.

**So: stop treating it as a funnel into HBA, and treat it as its own revenue line.**

|  | Era of Women | HBA |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Buyer | The woman herself | The company |
| Ticket | Lower | Higher |
| Cycle | Weeks | Months |
| Volume | Higher | Lower |
| Sells on | Resonance | Diagnosis and method |

Two lines, two speeds, one worldview above both. Era of Women gives you cash flow and audience velocity while HBA's longer cycles mature. It stops being a detour and becomes the fast lane.

**The one crossover to actively work:** women who are themselves founder-CEOs of 10–150-person companies. That group is both — and for them, Era of Women content _is_ the HBA entrance door.

<Warning>
  **What would change my mind.** If over the next two months you can name three women who came in through Era-of-Women content and ended up in a paid HBA conversation, then it _is_ the entrance door and we restructure around it. Track it — don't guess. Until then, run both lanes.
</Warning>

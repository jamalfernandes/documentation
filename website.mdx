## Read this first — the brief was wrong

You told me the site was badly outdated: _"it changed so much since I created it."_

I went through the live site line by line. **It hasn't.** You rebuilt it, and it's already aligned with the reframe we did in July. The site already has:

- **Human Ecology as the roof**, not HBA — the hero is _"L'écologie humaine / Un nouveau design pour la société"_
- **La juste place as the core message**, stated in the second paragraph on the page
- **All four pillars present** — Dirigeants, L'ère des femmes, Éducation, and the Lab carrying the Omara Soma work
- **A full manifesto** in five parts that is, honestly, some of the best writing you have
- **A productised three-level offer ladder** with named phases, durations, deliverables and J\+15/J\+30/J\+60/J\+90 follow-ups

<Note>
  **A correction I owe you.** I wrote that your competitors have no repeatable system, that you could productise your diagnostic, and that it was "worth designing once the case studies exist." **That was wrong — you have already built it.** The Diagnostic d'Alignement, the Diagnostic Système Dirigeant and the Compagnonnage are exactly the named, repeatable, phased system that Cristofini, del Marmol and Riot do not have. You are not behind where I thought you were. You are ahead of it.
</Note>

**So this is not a rewrite. It's a repair.** Five real problems, in order of how much damage they're doing.

## The five problems

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Problem 1 — The site is advertising an event from three months ago" icon="triangle-alert">
    **Urgent.** The only thing on the site that actively costs you credibility.

    The Programmes section still reads: _"Nouveau programme — Début 9 juin," "4 mardis consécutifs en juin," "Tarif lancement jusqu'au 31 mai : 444 €."_

    A visitor lands on a live page selling a launch price that expired eleven weeks ago, for a programme that started in June. It reads as abandoned — and "abandoned site" is the worst possible signal for someone deciding whether to trust you with their company.

    **Fix today, before anything else.** Three options, in order of preference:

    1. **Re-run it with new dates.** Copy is written, payment link works, bonus is built. Change five dates and one price.
    2. **Convert it to evergreen** — remove all dates, sell it as a recorded programme at a fixed price.
    3. **Take it down** until you re-run it.

    Don't leave it as is for another day.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Problem 2 — Your best commercial asset is buried" icon="eye-off">
    The three-level offer ladder is the strongest thing on the site and the strongest thing you own commercially. A visitor has to travel through the hero, the vision, three doors, "mon travail", the e-book, a five-part manifesto, "créer des systèmes vertueux", the ère-des-femmes field and the éducation field before reaching it.

    **A founder-CEO with a real problem gives you thirty seconds. They will not find it.**

    Three changes, no rewriting required:

    1. **Reorder.** Dirigeants moves directly after the vision section. Manifesto, ère des femmes and éducation move below it. The manifesto is your depth — depth goes _after_ the door, not in front of it. That's the sidewalk rule applied to page order.
    2. **Make the hero CTA go somewhere specific.** Point it straight at the ladder.
    3. **Put the ladder's three names in the nav** or as an anchor row: _Lecture stratégique · Système dirigeant · Compagnonnage._ Three words each; a CEO can orient in one glance.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Problem 3 — The e-book contradicts the entire email strategy" icon="unlink">
    Your lead magnet block promises _"Aucune liste de diffusion. Pas de relances commerciales."_

    Meanwhile [Email sequences](/emails) builds three sequences on the assumption that people who come in through the site get emailed. **These cannot both be true.** Right now the promise wins — and it means your only lead-capture mechanism is explicitly forbidden from ever contacting anyone.

    I understand where the line came from. It's the anti-marketing instinct, and it's the same instinct that makes you trustworthy. But it's costing you the whole funnel.

    **Proposed replacement:**

    ```text
    Being, before entrepreneur
    A mini e-book — and a first diagnostic.
    
    A few questions to ask yourself, to step now into what an
    aligned company looks like: a structure able to carry the
    full scale of your vision.
    
    [Receive the e-book]
    
    No sales sequence. One idea a week, and you can leave
    whenever you want.
    ```

    That last line is the whole fix. You're not promising silence — you're promising _no pressure_, which is what you actually meant and what people actually care about.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Problem 4 — Voice drifts to the front door in three places" icon="door-open">
    Most of the site holds the sidewalk rule well. The Dirigeants section in particular is excellent — _"lecture stratégique du vivant," "désalignements invisibles," "systèmes vivants."_ That's exactly the register.

    **a) Your bio opens on the front door.** _"Elle est née d'un éveil profond à la conscience…"_ / _"J'ai vécu plusieurs années au sein d'une communauté d'éveil…"_ A CEO evaluating you reads this in the first fifteen lines. _Éveil_ is a **hold** word. The content stays; the order changes — see the rewrite below.

    **b) "Redevenez Vivant" is written for a different reader.** _"Cessez de vous réduire," "reconnecter avec votre vitalité"_ — correct for Omara Soma, wrong on the same scroll as the diagnostic. Not a copy problem, a structure problem. It belongs behind the Programmes door.

    **c) "Stratège en scaling humain"** — **keep this, and promote it.** "Scaling humain" is the most commercially precise phrase on your entire site. Business-native, says what you do, no competitor uses it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Problem 5 — No price anywhere on the leadership ladder" icon="tag">
    "Redevenez Vivant" shows 444 €. The three diagnostic levels show nothing — every one ends in _"Premier échange."_

    Clear pricing is one of your four structural answers to the _dérive sectaire_ [objection](/objections). Its absence is felt exactly where the objection is strongest: the corporate buyer.

    Not full price lists — **anchors**. _"À partir de X"_ on each of the three levels. It qualifies people before they reach you, answers the objection silently, and is what a serious professional service does.

    **This needs your numbers.** See [Open items](/open-items).
  </Accordion>

## The rewrites

Only the blocks that need changing. Everything not listed here, leave alone.

### Hero — a small sharpening

**Keep the current hero.** It's right. One addition: the hero has no line telling a business visitor they're in the right place. Add a third line beneath.

```text
Human Ecology
A new design for society

For leaders whose company has outgrown the way it was built.
```

The worldview stays on top; the door gets named immediately underneath. Roof creates the pull, pillar catches the person — expressed in three lines.

### The core message — keep, with one cut

The current French is genuinely good and the clearest statement of _la juste place_ anywhere in your material. One edit: _"la vie recommence à circuler, les liens se retissent"_ is two images doing one job. Cut to one and the sentence lands harder.

```text
There is a right place for every person and every thing. When
that place isn't respected, human systems fall out of alignment,
harden, exhaust themselves, and produce symptoms. When it's found
again, the system starts to breathe — and it can grow and renew
itself naturally.
```

### The three doors — currently four things called three

"Trois portes d'entrée" lists Dirigeants, Programmes and La vision. But the page also has ère des femmes and éducation as their own fields, so the visitor meets five entry points while being told there are three.

The house has four pillars. The site should say four, and it should map to [the self-sorting map](/audiences).

```text
Four ways in

For leaders — A strategic diagnostic and in-depth support for
heads of small and mid-sized companies who feel their company
no longer breathes the way it used to, and want to understand
what to realign first.

Programmes — The Human Ecology Lab and the live programmes: a
living space to explore and realign, for those who want to walk
this reading of the living through their own path.

The era of women — Women taking their right place, and what
changes in a society when they do.

Education — Another way of reading the children we call
difficult, and the schools and parents around them.

And underneath all four: the vision.
```

### The bio — reordered, not rewritten

The material is good. The sequence buries the credibility and leads with the hold-words.

```text
Armelle Six
Human scaling and vision alignment

"Some visions aren't asking to be made smaller. They're asking
for the walls to come down so they can exist."

My name is Armelle. For more than fifteen years I've been working
on a single question: how do we put the living back at the centre
of our human systems?

In 2008, after a conference on ecology and everything going wrong
in our society, one thing became obvious to me: we will never
build a sustainable world without putting the human being back at
the centre — because a human being cut off from themselves cannot
build living systems.

That was the night Human Ecology was born.

At the time I had neither the words nor the structures to explain
it. But I already knew this vision would one day have to enter
companies, and change the way we lead, work, and build.
```

**Then, and only then, the path:**

```text
The years that followed took me down a long road of
transformation. I spent several years inside a community devoted
to that work, exploring consciousness, movement, the body, human
dynamics, and the capacity of the living to reorganise itself
once you stop constraining it.

Out of that came Movement of Life, Be&Move, work in embodied
mindfulness, conscious parenting, and spaces of transformation
that reached thousands of people over more than fifteen years.

No advertising. No conventional marketing. Only real
transformation, word of mouth, and living transmission.
```

<Note>
  **What changed and why it matters.** Nothing was cut — every fact survives. But a CEO now meets the question, the origin and the mission before they meet _éveil_ and the community. By the time they reach that paragraph, they've already decided you're serious, and the depth reads as substance instead of as a warning sign.

  That closing line is one of the strongest sentences on the site. It is the proof behind 12.4k subscribers and 863k views. **Keep it exactly.**
</Note>

### The offer ladder — framing only

The three levels are well built. Don't touch the phases, formats or deliverables. Two additions:

```text
Three levels.
One   — we understand what's blocking. Just you.
Two   — we understand what's blocking, then align the key
        people so it moves. You and your leadership team.
Three — we accompany the realignment over time.

Most people start at level one.
```

That last line removes the choice paralysis and points at the entry product. Then **price anchors** — _"from X"_ on each level. Blocked on your numbers.

### The manifesto — leave it alone

No changes. It is on-voice, it earns the depth it takes, and it is the piece of writing that most sounds like you. Its only problem is position, not content.

One structural suggestion: it's strong enough to be **its own page**, linked as _"Read the manifesto"_ rather than sitting inline. That keeps the main scroll commercial and gives the manifesto a URL people can send each other — which is what actually happens with writing this good.

## The lead page you asked for

You said you want a lead page and don't know what to put on it. The answer is smaller than you think: **you already have one — it's just embedded and defanged.**

The "Être avant d'être entrepreneur" block is a lead page in miniature. It has the right asset (a mini-diagnostic, which is a preview of the actual product), the right audience, and a working capture form. What it doesn't have is a URL of its own or permission to follow up.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Headline">
    The diagnostic promise. _Being, before entrepreneur._
  </Step>
  <Step title="Three or four lines">
    On what the e-book actually asks you — this is the preview of the paid diagnostic.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The form">
    One field, email only. Never more on a lead page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Two lines on who you are">
    Fifteen years, five books, the scaling-humain line. Not the bio.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The honest promise">
    _No sales sequence. One idea a week. Leave whenever._
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  **This closes the loop on the whole funnel:** the show and [the ads](/ads) bring people to the lead page, the e-book previews the diagnostic, [the welcome sequence](/emails) sorts them into a pillar, and the diagnostic is the paid door. Every piece already exists. The only genuinely missing part was permission to email — Problem 3.
</Info>

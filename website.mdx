## Read this first: the brief was wrong

You told me the site was badly outdated: "it changed so much since I created it."

I went through the live site line by line. It hasn't. You rebuilt it, and it's already aligned with the reframe we did in July. The site already has:

- Human Ecology as the roof, not HBA. The hero is "L'écologie humaine / Un nouveau design pour la société"
- _La juste place_ as the core message, stated in the second paragraph on the page
- All four pillars present: Dirigeants, L'ère des femmes, Éducation, and the Lab carrying the Omara Soma work
- A full manifesto in five parts that is, honestly, some of the best writing you have
- A productised three-level offer ladder with named phases, durations, deliverables and J\+15/J\+30/J\+60/J\+90 follow-ups

<Note>
  A correction I owe you. I wrote that your competitors have no repeatable system, that you could productise your diagnostic, and that it was "worth designing once the case studies exist." That was wrong. You have already built it. The Diagnostic d'Alignement, the Diagnostic Système Dirigeant and the Compagnonnage are exactly the named, repeatable, phased system that Cristofini, del Marmol and Riot do not have. You are not behind where I thought you were. You are ahead of it.
</Note>

So this is not a rewrite. It's a repair. Five real problems, in order of how much damage they're doing.

## The five problems

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Problem 1: the site is advertising an event from three months ago" icon="triangle-alert">
    Urgent, and still open. The only thing on the site that actively costs you credibility.

    The Programmes section still reads: "Nouveau programme, début 9 juin," "4 mardis consécutifs en juin," "Tarif lancement jusqu'au 31 mai : 444 €."

    A visitor lands on a live page selling a launch price that expired in May, for a programme that started in June. It reads as abandoned, and an abandoned site is the worst possible signal for someone deciding whether to trust you with their company.

    Three options, in order of preference:

    1. Re-run it with new dates. Copy is written, payment link works, bonus is built. Change five dates and one price.
    2. Convert it to evergreen. Remove all dates, sell it as a recorded programme at a fixed price.
    3. Take it down until you re-run it.

    This is the cheapest fix available and it is worth doing before anything else on this page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Problem 2: your best commercial asset is buried" icon="eye-off">
    The three-level offer ladder is the strongest thing on the site and the strongest thing you own commercially. A visitor has to travel through the hero, the vision, three doors, "mon travail", the e-book, a five-part manifesto, "créer des systèmes vertueux", the ère-des-femmes field and the éducation field before reaching it.

    A founder-CEO with a real problem gives you thirty seconds. They will not find it.

    Three changes, no rewriting required:

    1. Reorder. Dirigeants moves directly after the vision section. Manifesto, ère des femmes and éducation move below it. The manifesto is your depth, and depth goes after the door, not in front of it. That's the sidewalk rule applied to page order.
    2. Make the hero CTA go somewhere specific. Point it straight at the ladder.
    3. Put the ladder's three names in the nav or as an anchor row: Lecture stratégique · Système dirigeant · Compagnonnage. Three words each, so a CEO can orient in one glance.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Problem 3: the lead magnet changes, and the promise still contradicts the emails" icon="unlink">
    Two things here, and the first one now has an answer.

    **The lead magnet becomes a diagnostic questionnaire.** Decided on 14 August, and the evidence for it is the best you have: the client who paid in full that week converted because he read the small booklet on your site, recognised his own situation point by point, and got in touch. That is the format working. A short interactive questionnaire ("do you have this in your company? and this?") makes it concrete for the reader and captures the lead at the same time. Build it in something like Typeform and embed it on the page.

    In the meantime, the show is the better interim lead magnet than the e-book. Embed the YouTube episodes on the site and let the CTAs do the work.

    **The promise is still unresolved.** Your lead magnet block promises "Aucune liste de diffusion. Pas de relances commerciales." Meanwhile [Email sequences](/emails) builds three sequences on the assumption that people who come in through the site get emailed. These cannot both be true, and right now the promise wins.

    I understand where the line came from. It's the anti-marketing instinct, and it's the same instinct that makes you trustworthy. But it's costing you the whole funnel, and a questionnaire that captures an email and then may never use it is a strange thing to build.

    Proposed replacement:

    ```text
    Being, before entrepreneur
    A short diagnostic.
    
    A few questions to ask yourself, to see what an aligned
    company looks like: a structure able to carry the full
    scale of your vision.
    
    [Start the diagnostic]
    
    No sales sequence. One idea a week, and you can leave
    whenever you want.
    ```

    That last line is the whole fix. You're not promising silence. You're promising no pressure, which is what you actually meant and what people actually care about.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Problem 4: voice drifts to the front door in three places" icon="door-open">
    Most of the site holds the sidewalk rule well. The Dirigeants section in particular is excellent: "lecture stratégique du vivant," "désalignements invisibles," "systèmes vivants." That's exactly the register.

    a) Your bio opens on the front door. "Elle est née d'un éveil profond à la conscience..." and "J'ai vécu plusieurs années au sein d'une communauté d'éveil..." A CEO evaluating you reads this in the first fifteen lines. _Éveil_ is a hold word. The content stays, the order changes. See the rewrite below.

    b) "Redevenez Vivant" is written for a different reader. "Cessez de vous réduire," "reconnecter avec votre vitalité." Correct for Omara Soma, wrong on the same scroll as the diagnostic. Not a copy problem, a structure problem. It belongs behind the Programmes door.

    c) "Stratège en scaling humain." Keep this one, and promote it. "Scaling humain" is the most commercially precise phrase on your entire site. Business-native, says what you do, no competitor uses it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Problem 5: pricing. Decided, prices stay off the site" icon="tag">
    **Closed on 14 August.** Prices are not going on the website. Your reason: you want to stay premium, and premium doesn't publish prices. That's a legitimate position and plenty of serious professional services take it.

    What it costs you is one of the four structural answers to the _dérive sectaire_ [objection](/objections), so the job moves off the page and onto the conversation. Two things make up the difference:

    1. **Answer the price question immediately when it comes, without hedging.** The damage is done by having no answer, not by declining to publish. "The diagnostic is €1,500. The immersion is €7,500, scoped from what the diagnostic finds, and it runs over three months." That is a clear, confident answer, and it sounds premium rather than evasive.
    2. **Put the qualifying work into the diagnostic questionnaire instead.** Published anchors were doing a filtering job. The questionnaire does that job better, because someone who has just recognised their own situation point by point arrives already convinced there's something to fix.

    The figures themselves are on [Open items](/open-items), for internal reference.
  </Accordion>

## The rewrites

Only the blocks that need changing. Everything not listed here, leave alone.

### Hero: a small sharpening

Keep the current hero. It's right. One addition: the hero has no line telling a business visitor they're in the right place. Add a third line beneath.

```text
Human Ecology
A new design for society

For leaders whose company has outgrown the way it was built.
```

The worldview stays on top, the door gets named immediately underneath. Roof creates the pull, pillar catches the person, expressed in three lines.

### The core message: keep, with one cut

The current French is genuinely good and the clearest statement of _la juste place_ anywhere in your material. One edit: "la vie recommence à circuler, les liens se retissent" is two images doing one job. Cut to one and the sentence lands harder.

```text
There is a right place for every person and every thing. When
that place isn't respected, human systems fall out of alignment,
harden, exhaust themselves, and produce symptoms. When it's found
again, the system starts to breathe, and it can grow and renew
itself naturally.
```

### The three doors: currently four things called three

"Trois portes d'entrée" lists Dirigeants, Programmes and La vision. But the page also has ère des femmes and éducation as their own fields, so the visitor meets five entry points while being told there are three.

The house has four pillars. The site should say four, and it should map to [the self-sorting map](/audiences).

```text
Four ways in

For leaders. A strategic diagnostic and in-depth support for
heads of small and mid-sized companies who feel their company
no longer breathes the way it used to, and want to understand
what to realign first.

Programmes. The Human Ecology Lab and the live programmes: a
living space to explore and realign, for those who want to walk
this reading of the living through their own path.

The era of women. Women taking their right place, and what
changes in a society when they do.

Education. Another way of reading the children we call
difficult, and the schools and parents around them.

And underneath all four: the vision.
```

### The bio: reordered, not rewritten

The material is good. The sequence buries the credibility and leads with the hold-words.

```text
Armelle Six
Human scaling and vision alignment

"Some visions aren't asking to be made smaller. They're asking
for the walls to come down so they can exist."

My name is Armelle. For more than fifteen years I've been working
on a single question: how do we put the living back at the centre
of our human systems?

In 2008, after a conference on ecology and everything going wrong
in our society, one thing became obvious to me: we will never
build a sustainable world without putting the human being back at
the centre, because a human being cut off from themselves cannot
build living systems.

That was the night Human Ecology was born.

At the time I had neither the words nor the structures to explain
it. But I already knew this vision would one day have to enter
companies, and change the way we lead, work, and build.
```

Then, and only then, the path:

```text
The years that followed took me down a long road of
transformation. I spent several years inside a community devoted
to that work, exploring consciousness, movement, the body, human
dynamics, and the capacity of the living to reorganise itself
once you stop constraining it.

Out of that came Movement of Life, Be&Move, work in embodied
mindfulness, conscious parenting, and spaces of transformation
that reached thousands of people over more than fifteen years.

No advertising. No conventional marketing. Only real
transformation, word of mouth, and living transmission.
```

<Note>
  What changed and why it matters. Nothing was cut. Every fact survives. But a CEO now meets the question, the origin and the mission before they meet _éveil_ and the community. By the time they reach that paragraph, they've already decided you're serious, and the depth reads as substance instead of as a warning sign.

  That closing line is one of the strongest sentences on the site. It is the proof behind 12.4k subscribers and 863k views. Keep it exactly.
</Note>

### The offer ladder: framing only

The three levels are well built. Don't touch the phases, formats or deliverables. One addition:

```text
Three levels.
One.   We understand what's blocking. Just you.
Two.   We understand what's blocking, then align the key
       people so it moves. You and your leadership team.
Three. We accompany the realignment over time.

Most people start at level one.
```

That last line removes the choice paralysis and points at the entry product. No prices on the page, per Problem 5.

### Client proof: the thing the ladder has been missing

You now have [three documented cases](/proof). The site carries a fully built offer ladder and not one word of proof anywhere on it, which is the most visible remaining gap.

Keep them anonymised, as your own document requires. A short block under the ladder, three or four lines per case, in the form the cases already take: the problem as presented, what the diagnostic revealed, what moved. No figures, no claims of finished transformation.

### The manifesto: leave it alone

No changes. It is on-voice, it earns the depth it takes, and it is the piece of writing that most sounds like you. Its only problem is position, not content.

One structural suggestion: it's strong enough to be its own page, linked as "Read the manifesto" rather than sitting inline. That keeps the main scroll commercial and gives the manifesto a URL people can send each other, which is what actually happens with writing this good.

## The lead page

You said you want a lead page and don't know what to put on it. As of 14 August the answer is clearer: it's the diagnostic questionnaire, on its own page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Headline">
    The diagnostic promise. _Being, before entrepreneur._
  </Step>
  <Step title="The questionnaire">
    A short set of concrete questions. Do you have this in your company? And this? This is the part that converts, because the reader diagnoses themselves before you ever speak.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The capture">
    Email at the end, not the start. They've already invested attention by then.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Two lines on who you are">
    Fifteen years, five books, the scaling-humain line. Not the bio.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The honest promise">
    No sales sequence. One idea a week. Leave whenever.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  This closes the loop on the whole funnel. The show and [the ads](/ads) bring people to the lead page, the questionnaire has them recognise their own situation, [the welcome sequence](/emails) sorts them into a pillar, and the diagnostic is the paid door. Every piece now exists or is being built. The only genuinely missing part is permission to email, which is Problem 3.
</Info>

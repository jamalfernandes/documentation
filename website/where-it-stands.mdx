## The thing that matters most

You have a **productised three-level offer ladder** — the Diagnostic d'Alignement, the Diagnostic Systeme Dirigeant plus Journee d'Alignement, and the Compagnonnage. Named phases. Durations. Deliverables. Follow-ups at 15, 30, 60 and 90 days.

<Note>
  In the competitive work I wrote that your competitors have no repeatable system, that you could productise your diagnostic, and that it was worth designing later.

  **That was wrong. You have already built it.** It's live on your site right now, with a level of operational detail that Cristofini, del Marmol and Riot don't come close to.

  You are not behind where I thought you were. You are ahead of it.
</Note>

## So this is a repair, not a rewrite

Nothing needs throwing away. What the site has wrong is **sequence, emphasis, and one badly stale section** — plus two things it's missing: a lead page, and visible pricing.

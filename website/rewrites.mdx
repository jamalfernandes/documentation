Only the blocks that need changing are here. Anything not listed, I'd leave alone.

## Hero — a small sharpening

**Current:** _L'ecologie humaine / Un nouveau design pour la societe_

**Keep it.** It's right. One addition: the hero has no line telling a business visitor they're in the right place. Add a third line underneath:

> **Human Ecology** **A new design for society**
>
> _For leaders whose company has outgrown the way it was built._

The worldview stays on top; the door gets named immediately underneath. Roof creates the pull, pillar catches the person — in three lines.

## Core message — keep, with one cut

This is genuinely good, and it's the clearest statement of _la juste place_ anywhere in your material. Keep it almost intact.

One edit: _"la vie recommence a circuler, les liens se retissent"_ is two images doing one job. Cut to one and the sentence lands harder.

> There is a right place for every person and every thing. When that place isn't respected, human systems fall out of alignment, harden, exhaust themselves, and produce symptoms. When it's found again, the system starts to breathe — and it can grow and renew itself naturally.

## Three doors becomes four

_"Trois portes d'entree"_ currently lists Dirigeants, Programmes and La vision. But the page also has ere des femmes and education as their own fields — so a visitor meets five entry points while being told there are three.

The house has four pillars. The site should say four, and it should match the self-sorting map.

> **Four ways in**
>
> **For leaders** — A strategic diagnostic and in-depth support for heads of small and mid-sized companies who feel their company no longer breathes the way it used to, and want to understand what to realign first.
>
> **Programmes** — The Human Ecology Lab and the live programmes: a living space to explore and realign, for those who want to walk this reading of the living through their own path.
>
> **The era of women** — Women taking their right place, and what changes in a society when they do.
>
> **Education** — Another way of reading the children we call difficult, and the schools and parents around them.
>
> _And underneath all four: the vision._

## The bio — reordered, not rewritten

The material is good. The sequence buries the credibility and leads with the hold-words.

**Open here instead:**

> **Armelle Six** _Human scaling and vision alignment_
>
> "Some visions aren't asking to be made smaller. They're asking for the walls to come down so they can exist."
>
> My name is Armelle. For more than fifteen years I've been working on a single question: how do we put the living back at the centre of our human systems?
>
> In 2008, after a conference on ecology and everything going wrong in our society, one thing became obvious to me: we will never build a sustainable world without putting the human being back at the centre — because a human being cut off from themselves cannot build living systems.
>
> That was the night Human Ecology was born.
>
> At the time I had neither the words nor the structures to explain it. But I already knew this vision would one day have to enter companies, and change the way we lead, work, and build.

**Then, and only then, the path:**

> The years that followed took me down a long road of transformation. I spent several years inside a community devoted to that work, exploring consciousness, movement, the body, human dynamics, and the capacity of the living to reorganise itself once you stop constraining it.
>
> Out of that came Movement of Life, Be&Move, work in embodied mindfulness, conscious parenting, and spaces of transformation that reached thousands of people over more than fifteen years.
>
> No advertising. No conventional marketing. Only real transformation, word of mouth, and living transmission.

<Note>
  **What changed.** Nothing was cut — every fact survives. But a CEO now meets the question, the origin and the mission before they meet _eveil_ and the community. By the time they reach that paragraph, they've already decided you're serious, and the depth reads as substance instead of as a warning sign.

  That closing line — _no advertising, no conventional marketing, only word of mouth_ — is one of the strongest sentences on the site. It's the proof behind 12.4k subscribers and 863k views. Keep it exactly.
</Note>

## The offer ladder — framing only

The three levels are well built. Don't touch the phases, formats or deliverables. Two additions:

**A single orienting line above the ladder**, so a visitor knows which is theirs before reading three long blocks:

> **Three levels.** **One** — we understand what's blocking. Just you. **Two** — we understand what's blocking, then align the key people so it moves. You and your leadership team. **Three** — we accompany the realignment over time.
>
> Most people start at level one.

That last line does real work — it removes the choice paralysis and points at the entry product.

**And price anchors.** _A partir de X_ on each level.

## The manifesto — leave it alone

No changes. It's on-voice, it earns the depth it takes, and it's the piece of writing that most sounds like you. Its only problem is position, not content.

<Tip>
  One suggestion: it's strong enough to be its own page, linked as _"Lire le manifeste"_ rather than sitting inline. That keeps the main scroll commercial, and gives the manifesto a URL people can send each other — which is what actually happens with writing this good.
</Tip>

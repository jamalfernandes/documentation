## 1. The site is advertising an event from three months ago

<Warning>
  **Fix this today, before anything else here.**

  The programmes section still reads: _"Nouveau programme — Debut 9 juin"_ · _"4 mardis consecutifs en juin"_ · _"Tarif lancement jusqu'au 31 mai : 444 EUR"_ · _"Tarif apres le 31 mai : 800 EUR."_

  It is mid-August. A visitor lands on a live page selling a launch price that expired eleven weeks ago, for a programme that started in June. It reads as abandoned — and "abandoned site" is the worst possible signal for someone deciding whether to trust you with their company.
</Warning>

Three ways out, in order of preference:

1. **Re-run it with new dates.** The copy is written, the payment link works, the bonus is built. Change five dates and one price. That's an afternoon's work for a live offer.
2. **Make it evergreen** — remove all dates, sell it as a recorded programme at a fixed price with the replays.
3. **Take it down** until you re-run it, and let the Lab carry the programmes section alone.

## 2. Your best commercial asset is buried

The three-level ladder is the strongest thing on the site. To reach it, a visitor travels through the hero, the vision, three doors, _mon travail_, the e-book, a five-part manifesto, _creer des systemes vertueux_, the ere-des-femmes field and the education field.

A founder-CEO with a real problem gives you thirty seconds. They will not find it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reorder">
    Dirigeants moves directly after the vision section. Manifesto, ere des femmes and education move below it. The manifesto is your depth — and depth goes _after_ the door, not in front of it. That's the sidewalk rule applied to page order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make the hero CTA go somewhere specific">
    "Pour les dirigeants" currently leads into a long section. Point it straight at the ladder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Put the three level names in the navigation">
    _Lecture strategique · Systeme dirigeant · Compagnonnage._ Three words each. A CEO can orient in one glance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 3. The e-book contradicts the email strategy

Your lead magnet block promises:

> _"Aucune liste de diffusion. Pas de relances commerciales."_

Meanwhile the [email sequences](/email) are built on the assumption that people who come in through the site get emailed.

**Both cannot be true.** Right now the promise wins — which means your only lead-capture mechanism on the entire site is formally forbidden from ever contacting anyone. You collect emails and then undertake never to use them.

I understand where the line came from. It's the anti-marketing instinct, and it's part of why people trust you. But it's costing you the whole funnel.

<Tip>
  **Proposed replacement — keeps the promise honest, opens the door:**

  > No sales sequence. One idea a week, and you can leave whenever you want.

  That's the whole fix. You're not promising silence — you're promising _no pressure_, which is what you actually meant and what people actually care about. It's more honest than the current line, because the current line is a promise you'll eventually want to break.
</Tip>

If you'd rather keep the strict version, say so — and the email work gets rebuilt around the show and your existing 10,000 only.

## 4. Voice drifts to the front door in three places

Most of the site holds the sidewalk rule well. The Dirigeants section is genuinely excellent — _lecture strategique du vivant, desalignements invisibles, systemes vivants._ Spiritual truth in language a CEO accepts. That's exactly the register.

Three places break it:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Your bio opens on the front door" icon="user">
    A CEO evaluating you reads _"eveil de conscience"_ and _"communaute d'eveil"_ in the first fifteen lines. _Eveil_ is a **hold** word — sidewalk or private, not the front door of your own bio.

    The content stays. Only the order changes. See [the rewrites](/website/rewrites).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Redevenez Vivant is written for a different reader" icon="users">
    _Cessez de vous reduire, reconnecter avec votre vitalite, incarner ma grandeur_ — this is Omara Soma language, and it's correct for that audience. The problem is that it sits on the same page as the diagnostic, with no separation. A CEO scrolling past it recalibrates who you are, and not upward.

    Not a copy problem — a structure problem. It belongs behind the Programmes door.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Stratege en scaling humain — keep this one" icon="star">
    This is a genuine find. **"Scaling humain" is the most commercially precise phrase on your entire site.** Business-native, says what you do, no competitor uses it, and it translates the whole worldview into two words a founder already understands.

    It's stronger than _ecologie humaine_ as a **commercial** handle — which matters, given we're keeping the name.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## 5. No price anywhere on the leadership ladder

_Redevenez Vivant_ shows 444 EUR. The three diagnostic levels show nothing — every one ends in _"Premier echange."_

Clear pricing is one of your four structural answers to the _derive sectaire_ objection. Its absence is felt most exactly where that objection is strongest: the corporate buyer.

I'm not proposing full price lists — just **anchors.** _A partir de X_ on each of the three levels. It qualifies people before they reach you, answers the objection silently, and it's what a serious professional service does.

<Warning>
  This needs your numbers. It's the one thing genuinely blocking me.
</Warning>

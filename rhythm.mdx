This is the page that answers "I don't see what I'm doing." Everything else in this pack is reference. This is the actual week.

## The week

| Day | What | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Monday** | Record and publish the show. One guest a month, three episodes yourself. | Your existing block |
| **Tuesday** | Send the show email to the list, using the [four-block template](/emails). | 15 min |
| **Wednesday** | LinkedIn post, diagnosis (level 2). Pull a hook from [the hook bank](/messaging). | 20 min |
| **Thursday** | Send the episode to your video editor. Three to four clips. | 10 min |
| **Friday** | LinkedIn post, the way of seeing (level 1). | 20 min |
| **Ongoing** | Clips go out across Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn over the following two weeks. | Editor |
| **Monthly** | One offer post. Review what turned into conversations. | 30 min |

<Note>
  Under two hours a week of your own time, with the show already accounted for.
</Note>

## The one line you have never said

Every video, every episode, one line before the end:

> "If you want to go further, everything is on my website."

And the link in the description.

<Warning>
  You've never done this. It is the cheapest conversion improvement available to you, and YouTube is where your real audience lives: 12.4k subscribers, 863k lifetime views, 9:31 average view time.
</Warning>

## What to watch, monthly

How many episodes it takes to produce one conversation.

Right now the show has energy but doesn't bring clients, in your words. Once you know the ratio, say one client every three episodes, you know what content is worth, and we can start improving it deliberately instead of guessing.

## The habit that pays back the most

The Tuesday email. The show already has energy. The list already exists. Nothing currently connects them.

Fifteen minutes a week closes that gap. See [Email sequences](/emails) for the four-block template that makes it a fifteen-minute job rather than an hour.

This is the page that answers _"I don't see what I'm doing."_ Everything else in this pack is reference. **This is the actual week.**

| Day | What | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Monday** | Record and publish the show. One guest a month, three episodes yourself | Your existing block |
| **Tuesday** | Send the show email to the list — the four-block template | 15 min |
| **Wednesday** | LinkedIn post: diagnosis (level 2). Pull a hook from the bank | 20 min |
| **Thursday** | Send the episode to your video editor. Three to four clips | 10 min |
| **Friday** | LinkedIn post: the way of seeing (level 1) | 20 min |
| **Ongoing** | Clips go out across Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn over the following two weeks | Editor |
| **Monthly** | One offer post. Review what turned into conversations | 30 min |

<Note>
  **Under two hours a week of your own time**, with the show already accounted for.
</Note>

## The one line to add to every video

Before the end of every episode:

> "If you want to go further, everything is on my website."

And the link in the description.

You've never done this. It is the single cheapest conversion improvement available to you, and YouTube is where your real audience lives — 12.4k subscribers, 863k lifetime views, and an average view time of 9:31.

## What to watch, monthly

How many episodes it takes to produce one conversation.

Right now the show has energy but doesn't bring clients. Once you know the ratio — say, one client every three episodes — you know what a piece of content is actually worth, and we can start improving it deliberately instead of guessing.

<Tip>
  Don't optimise before you measure. Keep doing what you're doing for a few more cycles, then look at what the first conversations actually came from.
</Tip>

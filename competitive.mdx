You asked how to actually _use_ the two competitive assets. Here they are in practice.

<Note>
  **My honest view: if the rest of this pack is done well, you never need to mention a competitor.** This is Plan F — for when someone is comparing you side by side with someone else.
</Note>

## Asset 1 — the reversal

**The line, sharp:**

> Not a CEO who found meditation, but a teacher who spent fifteen years inside the thing they went looking for.

That version is intentionally confrontational. Use the sharp version only in writing, in your own voice, about yourself.

**In conversation, dilute it into story.** The story version says the same thing without putting anyone down — which is both more like you and more effective.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **When to use** | The credibility gate. Someone is weighing you against a well-known ex-executive |
| **The frame** | You are not a further-along version of the same journey. You are the source they went looking for |

## Asset 2 — no repeatable system

Every competitor in the field has the same structural weakness: no named, repeatable method. They sell the person in the room. No repeatable system means no guaranteed outcome.

<Tip>
  **And you have already built the answer.** Your site carries a three-level ladder — the Diagnostic d'Alignement, the Diagnostic Systeme Dirigeant plus Journee d'Alignement, and the Compagnonnage — with named phases, durations, deliverables and follow-ups at 15, 30, 60 and 90 days.

  That is a named, repeatable, productised system with more operational detail than anything Cristofini, del Marmol or Riot have published. **This asset isn't aspirational. It's live**, and it's the single biggest thing separating you from the field.

  Use it that way: when the comparison comes up, don't argue depth — show the ladder.
</Tip>

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **When to use** | With an operator-minded CEO who values consistency and process. Lands hard with that profile, and not at all with someone buying on charisma |
| **The frame** | "My peers sell a presence. I offer a diagnostic — a method, stages, and a result you can name" |

**The doctor question**, for someone genuinely stuck between you and someone better known:

> "Would you rather go to a doctor who's famous, or a doctor whose patients leave healthy?"

## Where this goes next

The one rung the ladder is missing is a **self-serve entry** — a structured self-scan a company completes before ever speaking to you.

You have the raw material already: the e-book _"Etre avant d'etre entrepreneur"_ is described on your own site as a mini-diagnostic. Turning that into a scored self-scan gives you a free top rung that feeds the paid ladder — and it's the thing none of your competitors can build, because they _are_ the product.

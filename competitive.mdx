You asked how to actually use the two competitive assets. Here they are in practice.

<Note>
  My honest view: if the rest of this pack is done well, you never need to mention a competitor. This is Plan F, for when someone is comparing you side by side with someone else.
</Note>

## Asset 1: the reversal

The line, sharp:

> Not a CEO who found meditation, but a teacher who spent fifteen years inside the thing they went looking for.

That version is intentionally confrontational.

<Warning>
  Use the sharp version only in writing, in your own voice, about yourself. In conversation, dilute it into story. See [Post 4](/posts) and [the credibility objection](/objections). The story version says the same thing without putting anyone down, which is both more like you and more effective.
</Warning>

When to use: the credibility gate. Someone is weighing you against a well-known ex-executive.

The frame: you are not a further-along version of the same journey. You are the source they went looking for.

## Asset 2: no repeatable system

Every competitor in the field has the same structural weakness. No named, repeatable method. They sell the person in the room, and no repeatable system means no guaranteed outcome.

And you have already built the answer. Your site carries a three-level ladder (the Diagnostic d'Alignement, the Diagnostic Système Dirigeant plus Journée d'Alignement, and the Compagnonnage) with named phases, durations, deliverables and J\+15/J\+30/J\+60/J\+90 follow-ups.

<Note>
  That is a named, repeatable, productised system with more operational detail than anything Cristofini, del Marmol or Riot have published. This asset isn't aspirational. It's live, and it's the biggest single thing separating you from the field.
</Note>

Use it that way. When the comparison comes up, don't argue depth. Show the ladder.

When to use: with an operator-minded CEO who values consistency and process. This lands hard with that profile, and not at all with someone buying on charisma.

The frame:

> "My peers sell a presence. I offer a diagnostic: a method, stages, and a result you can name."

## The doctor question

For someone genuinely stuck between you and someone better known:

> "Would you rather go to a doctor who's famous, or a doctor whose patients leave healthy?"

## Where this goes next

The one rung the ladder is missing is a self-serve entry: a structured self-scan a company completes before ever speaking to you.

You have the raw material already. The e-book "Être avant d'être entrepreneur" is described on your site as a mini-diagnostic. Turning that into a scored self-scan gives you a free top rung that feeds the paid ladder.

<Info>
  And it's the thing none of your competitors can build, because they are the product.
</Info>

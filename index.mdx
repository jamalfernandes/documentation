> **Human Ecology is a new design for society. It sees every human system as a living ecosystem that only does well when everything and everyone is in its right place. Armelle Six helps leaders find that right place, so their organisation can grow without losing its humanity — or theirs.**

## What this is, and what it is not

The Brand Foundation Audit answered _who you are_. You told me it didn't tell you _what to do on Monday morning_:

<Note>
  "I still don't understand how I'm supposed to do my communication based on the document you gave me, because I don't see what I'm doing. So I just do what I do usually."
</Note>

Fair. This pack is the fix. It is **not a strategy document**. It is a working reference you open when you are about to write something.

<Warning>
  **One rule before anything else.** Nothing here is a script you must follow word for word. It is a set of rails. If a sentence here doesn't sound like you, change it until it does — as long as the essence stays. You are the source; this is only the map.
</Warning>

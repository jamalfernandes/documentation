## Why this exists

The Brand Foundation Audit answered _who you are_. You told me it didn't tell you what to do on Monday morning:

> "I still don't understand how I'm supposed to do my communication based on the document you gave me, because I don't see what I'm doing. So I just do what I do usually."

Fair. This is the fix. It is not a strategy document — it's a reference you open when you're about to write something.

## One rule before anything else

Nothing here is a script you must follow word for word. It is a set of rails.

If a sentence here doesn't sound like you, change it until it does — as long as the essence stays. You are the source; this is only the map.

<Warning>
  **Language.** Everything here is drafted in English for review and approval. Once signed off, the ready-to-use assets — hooks, posts, emails, objection answers — get translated into French, which is where they'll actually be published.
</Warning>

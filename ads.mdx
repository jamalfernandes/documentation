## What the LinkedIn boost taught us

You boosted the post with your show replay: roughly **5,000 views, about 20 engagements** — and not even on the link. That's about a 0.4% engagement rate, on the most expensive ad platform there is.

It didn't fail because the content was wrong. It failed because LinkedIn's boost button is the bluntest instrument available, and LinkedIn inventory is priced for enterprise B2B lead-gen.

## The cost ladder

| Platform | Cost | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Meta** (Facebook \+ Instagram) | Cheapest | **Start here.** Biggest audience, most engaged, cheapest reach |
| **Google / YouTube** | Middle | Later. YouTube especially — you already have the content |
| **LinkedIn** | Most expensive | Only for a narrow, high-value HBA campaign, with real targeting. Never the boost button |

## Where to start

Three things worth promoting, in this order:

<Steps>
  <Step title="The show replay">
    You already do this — just on the wrong platform. Move it to Meta. Small daily budget, running continuously rather than in bursts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The new book">
    It hit bestseller on Amazon off your list alone. Meta ads to a book are the cheapest possible way to buy a new reader — and a reader is a warm lead who paid you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The lead page, once it exists">
    This is the one that eventually feeds HBA.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Targeting and budget

**Targeting.** French-speaking, 35–60, interests around conscious business, regenerative leadership, personal development, and the authors adjacent to your field. Then build a lookalike from your 10,000-person list — that will almost certainly outperform any interest targeting.

**Budget.** Start small and constant. A modest daily spend running for a month teaches you far more than a large burst over three days.

**What to measure.** Not views. Cost per link click, and then what those clicks do. The LinkedIn boost bought views and nothing else — that's the exact trap to avoid repeating.

<Warning>
  **The honest caveat.** Ads amplify something that already converts. Right now nothing in your funnel is proven to convert — there's no lead page, and the show doesn't yet bring clients, by your own account.

  So treat this phase as **buying data, not buying clients.** Once the lead page exists and we know what a visitor does, ads become an investment rather than a test.
</Warning>
